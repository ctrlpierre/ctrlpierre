#### Audio & Research (IRIT Internship)
* **[Inverse Audio Synthesis](https://github.com/ctrlpierre/synth-param-inference)**: Design of a deep learning model to infer synthesizer parameters directly from an audio source file.
    * *Context: Research internship at the Institut de Recherche en Informatique de Toulouse (IRIT).*
    * *Tools: Python, PyTorch, Pyo Library, 2D CNNs, DSP.*
    * *Goal: While current AI trends lean towards direct audio generation, this project proposes an alternative approach—a tool capable of translating an existing sound into a synthesizer patch. The objective is to give control back to artists, allowing them to shape their sounds and preserve their creative freedom.*
